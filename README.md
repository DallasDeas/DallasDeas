## A little about me

I'm an IT support professional building deeper skills in networking, systems administration, and cloud infrastructure. My work has taken me through end-user troubleshooting, business applications, and the practical question behind every ticket: **what is preventing this person from getting their work done?**

My projects follow that curiosity into the infrastructure. I build the environment, check how the pieces depend on each other, and document what happens when something stops working. A missing mapped drive, a failed domain join, or a monitoring interruption gives me something concrete to investigate.

📍 **I'm interested in IT support, network support, infrastructure support, and systems operations roles.** Long term, I'm working toward cloud and network engineering.

I try to leave behind notes that someone else could follow. That means recording what I checked and what changed, along with the screenshot of the working result.
